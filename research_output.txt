# Seattle Seahawks 2024 NFL Season Summary

## Overview
The Seattle Seahawks completed the 2024 NFL season with a respectable 10-7 record, finishing second in the NFC West division. This was a season of significant transition for the franchise as they hired Mike Macdonald as head coach, ending Pete Carroll's 14-year tenure at the helm.

## Coaching Change
The most notable change for the 2024 Seahawks was the arrival of Mike Macdonald as the team's new head coach. After 14 seasons under Pete Carroll's leadership, the organization opted for a new direction. Macdonald's first season showed promise but also revealed some interesting patterns, particularly the stark contrast between home and road performance.

## Home vs. Road Performance
One of the most unusual aspects of the 2024 Seahawks under Macdonald was their drastically different performance at home versus on the road:
- Home record: 4-8 (struggling at Lumen Field)
- Road record: 10-1 (one of the best road teams in the NFL)

This represented a significant departure from the Seahawks' historical reputation as having one of the strongest home-field advantages in the NFL.

## Key Players
The 2024 Seahawks were led by several standout performers:

- **Geno Smith** (Quarterback): Continued as the team's starting quarterback and demonstrated versatility by becoming the team's second-leading rusher with 222 yards.
  
- **Kenneth Walker III** (Running Back): Led the team in rushing with 493 yards, though the run game struggled at times for consistency.
  
- **DK Metcalf** (Wide Receiver): Remained a key offensive weapon in the passing game.
  
- **Jaxon Smith-Njigba** (Wide Receiver): Had breakout performances, including a notable game against the Los Angeles Rams with 180 yards and two touchdowns.

## Season Trajectory
The Seahawks showed promise throughout the season and were in playoff contention into the latter weeks of the schedule. They held the NFC West lead at one point, but ultimately fell short of making the playoffs by the end of Week 17.

## Looking Ahead
As the Seahawks enter the 2025 offseason, several key decisions await the front office:
- Geno Smith's contract situation (entering the final year of his deal)
- Continuing to build around Mike Macdonald's system
- Addressing the puzzling home performance issues
- Strengthening the running game which lacked consistency in 2024

The 10-7 record, while missing the playoffs, demonstrated that the Seahawks remain competitive in the NFC West even during a transitional coaching year.
